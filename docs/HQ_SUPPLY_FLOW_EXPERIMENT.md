## Paused and time-advanced game results

The player provided two 1.142 game saves. `Save8_10_2026_14_14_13` is labeled **ADC Test 8 - Paused Save** and was saved without unpausing. It retains the edited I. CORPS path flow `0.5`, HQ stock `(10,10,10,10)`, and group stores `(100,78.93917,100,100)`. `Save8_10_2026_14_14_56` is labeled **ADC Test 8 - Time Advanced 100 percent supplies ratio**; the player reports the HQ panel jumped to 100% almost immediately after unpausing. Its game-written path flow is `1`, HQ stock remains `(10,10,10,10)`, and group stores are `(100,79.50203,100,100)`. The other path supply-header values are unchanged except the saved retreat-angle representation in the paused save, which is equivalent modulo 360 degrees. Both saves passed 18 ADC creation-resave checks and nine town checks.

The full-file [supply comparison](../scripts/Compare-CampaignSupplyRecords.ps1) matched all 814 path records between these saves by the game's four-value identity, without relying on line position or ordinal ID. It found 52 changed flow values and 467 changed stock arrays after time advanced. Among the six previously created combat units, each retained flow `1` while some stock amount decreased slightly. I. CORPS alone in that displayed sample changed flow `0.5 → 1` while its own four stock values remained constant.

This validates the panel's saved flow link while paused and demonstrates that the game **recalculates** that flow during normal campaign time. A raw `paths.dat` `supplystate` edit is therefore transient and cannot serve as a durable user-selected starting supply value. The observed artillery store change and widespread combat stock changes are consistent with separate reserve/consumption calculations; they do not establish an exact distribution formula or editable stock initialization. No ADC supply write control follows from this test.
